Privacy Policy

Cliptx is designed to give you full creative control while protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect basic account information such as your name, email address, and profile details when you register. We also collect app usage data to improve performance and user experience.

2. Media & Content

Your videos, photos, and edited content remain under your control. Cliptx does not access, modify, or share your media files without your permission.

3. How We Use Information

Collected information is used to provide app functionality, improve editing tools, enhance security, and deliver a better user experience.

4. Data Storage & Security

We use secure servers and encrypted communication (HTTPS) to protect your information. Reasonable security measures are implemented to prevent unauthorized access.

5. Third-Party Services

Cliptx may use trusted third-party services such as authentication providers, analytics tools, or cloud storage. These providers follow their own privacy policies.

6. Permissions

Cliptx requests only necessary permissions such as storage, camera, and microphone access to enable video and photo editing features.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your account information. You may contact support for any privacy-related concerns.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected within the app along with an updated revision date.
